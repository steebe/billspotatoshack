About

Bill's Potato Shack is a small, family operation. Bill picks the potatoes. Bill's wife inspects the potatoes. Bill's kids count the potatoes, mostly correctly. Grandma Bill keeps the books, which are also about potatoes.

Every potato we sell is hand-checked to confirm it is, in fact, a potato. We don't sell carrots. We've never sold carrots. Please stop asking about carrots. We're proud of our quality, and we think it shows. Just look at that potato.
